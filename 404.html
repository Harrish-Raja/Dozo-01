FAQ

Questions families ask

What if there’s no Wi-Fi at home?

The light, beep and voice reminders work fully offline. Alerts are sent when the box reconnects, and a SIM version is planned for Year 2.

Does Grandpa need a smartphone?

No. He only opens the box. The app is for the family.

How long does the battery last?

Dozo is rechargeable, and the family app warns you before the battery runs low.

Can several family members get alerts?

The free app covers one caregiver. Family premium adds up to five, in any city or country.
